Overview

The SATs Hydrothermal Autoclave, with a durable nonmagnetic 316-stainless steel shell and a research-grade PTFE or PPL inner vessel, is available in sizes from 25 mL to 2500 mL. Its robust design ensures safety under high-pressure conditions, making it ideal for material synthesis, chemical reactions, and geological studies. Applications

  • Material SynthesisUtilized in the synthesis of nanomaterials, zeolites, and other advanced materials that require high-pressure and high-temperature conditions.
  • Geochemical and Geological StudiesEssential for simulating natural hydrothermal processes, aiding in the study of mineral formation and other geological phenomena.
  • Pharmaceutical ResearchUsed in the development and testing of new pharmaceuticals, especially where high-pressure and high-temperature conditions are required.
  • Environmental TestingAssists in the analysis of environmental samples, helping to understand the impact of high-pressure conditions on various substances.
  • Chemical ReactionsFacilitates hydrothermal and solvothermal reactions, allowing for the exploration of new chemical pathways and the creation of novel compounds.
