Applications

  • Materials and Metallurgical ResearchDesigned for evaluating thermal, structural, and mechanical behavior of metals, ceramics, and advanced materials.
  • Ceramic and Composite SinteringIdeal for densification and sintering of ceramic and composite materials at controlled high temperatures.
  • Thermal Stability and Oxidation StudiesFacilitates precise examination of oxidation resistance and degradation behavior of materials under thermal stress.
  • Ash Content DeterminationUsed for determining ash content in various materials, particularly in analytical and quality control laboratories.
  • Heat Treatment of Metals and AlloysSupports annealing, tempering, and other heat treatment processes essential for metallurgical applications.
