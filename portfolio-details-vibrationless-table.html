Key Specifications

Worktop

Mild Steel top with Stainless Steel (SS-304) cladding for durability and corrosion resistance. Honeycomb core enhances rigidity and damping. Black epoxy-powder-coated finish for long-lasting protection.

Frame / Support Structure

Heavy-duty mild steel frame with epoxy-powder-coated finish (matte black or custom color) for strength and corrosion resistance.

Isolation Legs

Precision air-spring dampers with rubber and steel housing to minimize vibrations. Anti-vibration pads at the base ensure stability.

Accessories (Optional)

Includes stainless steel leveling feet, adjustable shelves or utility racks, and an anti-static top surface for versatile laboratory use.
