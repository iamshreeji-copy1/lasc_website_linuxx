Overview

The SATS Sample Holder for Thin Films and Solar Cells is meticulously engineered to offer secure and precise positioning for thin films and solar cells during testing and characterization. With innovative spring contact protection and pixel switching capabilities, this holder ensures accurate measurements and enhanced sample protection.
